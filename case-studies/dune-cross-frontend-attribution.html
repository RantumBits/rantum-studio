Dune Cross-Frontend Attribution Engine

Analyzing deep trace call data to uncover hidden frontend routing and user behaviors across decentralized protocols.

Andrew Maury
• Case Study

The Challenge

The decentralized exchange (DEX) ecosystem routes billions of dollars in volume, but attributing that volume to the correct originating frontend—like the Uniswap UI, MetaMask Swaps, or Matcha—is notoriously difficult.

There is no standardized way for a frontend to "announce" itself on-chain. Furthermore, true retail volume is frequently obfuscated behind complex proxy contracts or drowned out by massive programmatic arbitrage and MEV bot activity. Without clear attribution, protocols cannot accurately determine which interfaces are actually driving organic user growth.

The Solution

By leveraging Dune Analytics' highly granular trace and call tables, we built a macro-level attribution engine capable of identifying trade origins across the entire DEX space. Because frontends use wildly disparate methods to track their own volume, our engine had to be highly adaptive.

Architectural Approach

  • Calldata Extraction: Parsing raw hex calldata to identify custom affiliate codes appended to the end of transaction payloads.
  • Signature & Contract Mapping: Building a dynamic registry that maps specific router functions or dedicated smart contract addresses exclusively to their parent frontends.
  • Bot Filtration via Trace Analysis: Parsing trace_address arrays and proxy contract interactions to mathematically separate organic retail flow from high-frequency "bot" volume.

Example SQL Extraction

Below is a conceptual example of how we extract frontend attribution codes while filtering out identified bot addresses and proxy noise:

-- Extracting frontend codes while filtering bot volume
SELECT 
    evt_tx_hash,
    evt_block_time,
    tx_from AS sender,
    -- Decode the last 4 bytes of calldata to identify the frontend router
    RIGHT(encode(call_data, 'hex'), 8) as frontend_id,
    amount_in,
    amount_out
FROM dex_protocol.router_calls
WHERE success = true
-- Filter known MEV/Arbitrage bot contracts
AND tx_from NOT IN (SELECT address FROM labels.mev_bots)
-- Ensure direct interaction (proxy filtration)
AND call_depth <= 2

The Impact

This comprehensive attribution model provided an unprecedented, macro-level view of retail versus bot volume across the entire decentralized exchange ecosystem.

By successfully mapping previously "unknown" transactions to their exact frontend origins, the model allowed protocols and analysts to accurately assess which interfaces were truly driving market share. This data fundamentally shifted how liquidity incentives, affiliate fees, and growth strategies are optimized across the Web3 space.
